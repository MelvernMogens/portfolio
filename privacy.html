Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy covers "Hermes Agent", a personal assistant tool that Melvern Mogens runs for his own Google account. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the account owner's permission, the app can read and manage Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides and Contacts for that same account, to carry out tasks the owner asks for (for example, adding a calendar reminder or finding a file).

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to perform the owner's requests.
  • The access token is stored locally on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
